How it started

One founder,
one conviction.

Waqar Ahmed has always been driven by one question: how can technical expertise and strategic intelligence solve problems that actually matter? A decade working across chemical engineering, EU sustainability regulation, climate finance and AI product development gave him a precise answer.

The sustainability transition is the defining business challenge of this decade. Most firms treat it as a compliance problem. Waqar saw it as a decision problem — and built NUMICS to address it differently.

NUMICS is not built around a service catalogue. It is built around a specific accumulation of expertise — regulatory depth, quantitative rigour, global project experience and AI-augmented intelligence — delivered directly, without hierarchy.
